Forward – Account & Data Deletion

App: Forward (formerly MCore Recall / RememberMe – Smart Reminders)
Developer: MCore Creative
Last updated: 23 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Forward account and associated data. You do not need to be signed in to request deletion through email.

Option 1: Delete your account in Forward

  1. Open the Forward app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile and open your account settings.
  3. Select Delete account and follow the confirmation instructions.

If the deletion option is unavailable or you cannot sign in, use Option 2 below.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send a request to mcorecreative@gmail.com with the subject Forward account deletion request. Mention the email address used for your Forward account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting any information. Never email your password or payment-card details.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted?

When your deletion request is verified and completed, Forward will delete the associated app account and account-linked information managed by Forward, including stored reminders, tasks, goals and preferences where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, financial or legal obligations, and backups may persist for a limited period under applicable retention policies.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the cloud account or all cloud data. Deleting your Forward account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

Subscriptions

To stop future subscription renewals, separately cancel your subscription in Google Play subscriptions. If you need help accessing your account, email us.
