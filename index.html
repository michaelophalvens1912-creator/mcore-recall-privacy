Forward – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Forward (formerly MCore Recall / RememberMe – Smart Reminders) is a reminders, planning and goal-tracking app published by MCore Creative. This privacy policy describes how information is handled in Forward. Contact: mcorecreative@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account details, such as your email address, account identifier and sign-in information used with Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create: reminders, notes, dates, times, goals, tasks, categories, completion status and preferences.
  • Subscription information from Google Play, such as product identifiers, purchase status and verification details used to confirm Premium access.
  • Technical information needed to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service, including limited diagnostic and security logs.
  • Permission-based information when you choose to use speech input or notifications.

Forward does not directly collect your payment card details; Google Play handles payments.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide reminders and planning tools, manage subscriptions and access, provide notifications you request, protect the app, troubleshoot errors and respond to support or deletion requests. Processing is based on provision of the service, applicable legal requirements, legitimate security interests and consent where applicable.

Storage and service providers

Forward uses Google Firebase services for authentication and backend functions, including server-side subscription verification and account data operations. Some data may be processed or stored on servers as well as on your device. Google Play processes purchases and renewals. These providers process data under their own applicable privacy and contractual terms and may use infrastructure outside your country, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers as needed to operate Forward or when legally required.

Microphone and notifications

If you choose voice input, Forward requests microphone access. The Android/Google speech-recognition service on your device may process spoken audio according to that service's settings and privacy practices. Forward uses the recognized text to prepare a reminder for your review. Forward may also request notification permission to deliver reminders and alerts you configure. You can manage permissions in Android settings.

Subscriptions

Forward provides paid access through Google Play subscriptions, with a free-trial offer for eligible users. Google Play determines offer eligibility and handles billing. Forward checks subscription entitlements through its backend. Cancelling your subscription does not by itself delete your account or reminders. Subscriptions are managed separately through Google Play.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while needed to provide the service and for applicable legal, accounting or security obligations. You can delete individual items in Forward and request account deletion using the app's account-deletion feature. If you cannot access the app, email mcorecreative@gmail.com to request deletion of your Forward account and associated information. We may verify account ownership before acting. Some records may be retained where legally required, and deletion from backups may take additional time. Uninstalling the app alone does not necessarily delete account data stored on servers.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection regarding your personal information, and withdraw consent where applicable. Contact mcorecreative@gmail.com. You may also complain to your competent data-protection authority.

Children's privacy

Forward is a general productivity app and is not specifically directed at children. Contact us if you believe a child's information requires review or deletion.

Policy changes

We may revise this privacy policy when the app or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be available here.
