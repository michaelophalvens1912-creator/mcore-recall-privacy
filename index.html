Privacy Policy for MCore Recall

Last updated: 27 July 2026

MCore Recall is developed and published by MCore Creative. This privacy policy explains how the MCore Recall Android application handles information.

Information we collect

MCore Recall does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Local reminder data

Reminder titles, notes, dates, times, categories, language preferences, and completion status are stored locally on the user's device.

This information is not uploaded to MCore Creative or stored on an external server.

Notifications and alarms

MCore Recall may request permission to display notifications and schedule reminders or alarms. These permissions are used only to notify the user about reminders they created.

Analytics and advertising

MCore Recall does not use advertising SDKs, behavioural tracking, or third-party analytics services.

Data sharing

MCore Recall does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Reminder information remains on the device until the user deletes an individual reminder, removes all local data through the application settings, clears the application data, or uninstalls MCore Recall.

Children's privacy

MCore Recall is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed at children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
